## Standing rule

A coding agent may add a row whose `basis` is a **clause**: a named section
of the WHATWG URL Standard, an RFC, UTS #46, or an accepted ADR/PRD that
settles the question under the posture named in the row. The repository's
design posture (AGENTS.md) makes that decision the agent's to take: the
conformant behavior ships as the default, and a behavior the standard calls
invalid is a bug even when it has shipped. Only a row whose `basis` is
`OWNER` needs the owner, and that is reserved for surfaces no standard
covers — presentation choices, cleaning policy, product scope. Each row names
the tracker carriers it discharges; the closure comment on those issues points
back here.

Columns: `basis` is a clause citation or `OWNER`; `posture` is the arm(s) the
ruling binds; `consequence` names what moves, by file, without restating it.

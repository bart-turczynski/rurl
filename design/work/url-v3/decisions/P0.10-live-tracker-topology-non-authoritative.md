# P0.10 — Live tracker topology is non-authoritative working state

## Decision (PROPOSED)

> This record is the agent's **proposed** disposition, offered for the owner to
> ratify, reject, or adjust. Nothing here is binding until the owner merges it.

### D-A. The rule being amended

P0.3 §3 froze topology after the snapshot:

> Topology (parent links / reparenting) is **frozen after the snapshot**;
> changing it requires a new decision record, so every structural change is
> auditable.

This is **withdrawn** as stated. It is replaced by D-B.

### D-B. Live parentage is working state; the frozen export is the topology

Within the `RURL-dorofzmb` subtree:

- **Live `fp` parent links are non-authoritative working state**, on exactly the
  same footing P0.3 §3 already grants live status transitions and comments: they
  are working notes. Regrooming the live tree — reparenting issues, creating
  sub-epics, moving leaves between them — requires **no decision record**.
- **The topology of record is the frozen export**,
  `design/work/url-v3/evidence/tracker-snapshot.json`, as of the
  `control_plane_snapshot` commit. Every normative reference to "the tracker's
  topology" means that file, unchanged from P0.3 §2.
- Auditability is preserved where it actually lives: the next re-export captures
  the then-current tree in full, and the delta against the previous snapshot is
  the audit trail. A record per intermediate reparent audits nothing the export
  does not already show.

### D-C. What still requires a record

Two topology facts remain record-governed, because they are claims about the
program rather than about tracker convenience:

1. **The root's placement.** `RURL-dorofzmb` is a top-level root epic (P0.3 §1,
   unchanged). Nesting it under another epic, or splitting the program across
   two roots, still requires a superseding P0-tier record — the export's
   completeness rule (P0.3 §2) is defined by reachability from this root, so
   moving it silently changes what the snapshot means.
2. **Anything that changes the snapshot itself.** Re-exporting and committing a
   new `tracker-snapshot.json` remains governed by P0.2 §§116-127: a new
   `v3/cp-snapshot-<n>` tag, a `registers/snapshots.log` row, and a manifest
   `sha256` / `issue_count` / `as_of_commit` update, all under owner merge.

### D-D. Rationale — the rule taxed a cost it could not detect

Three findings drove this:

- **Internal tension.** P0.3 §2 (lines 75-76) holds that "live tracker drift
  after the snapshot has no authority", and §4 that fp IDs are "pointers, not
  authority". A rule taxing changes to state the same record declares
  authority-free cannot also be load-bearing. D-B resolves the tension in the
  direction the rest of P0.3 already points.
- **Unenforceable by construction.** No validator or workflow reads live `fp`
  state: `ci-gate.R` runs `validate-records.R`, `validate-transitions.R` and
  `validate-manifest.R`, all file-hash checks against committed artifacts, and
  `control-plane.yml` triggers only on `design/work/url-v3/**` paths. The
  obligation was honor-system, so its only reliable effect was on velocity, not
  on auditability.
- **Measured cost.** The rule's practical effect was to discourage regrooming a
  40-plus-leaf flat epic, which is the condition it was meant to keep auditable.

### D-E. Ratification of the 2026-08-01 regrooming

The regrooming performed on 2026-08-01 is ratified under D-B and requires no
further record. It created five sub-epics under `RURL-dorofzmb` —
`RURL-fzotxfre` (gate mechanics), `RURL-cjaqeteq` (evidence & oracle
provenance), `RURL-gmocgnbi` (record errata), `RURL-dbnycxba` (remaining public
surface), `RURL-msyphacr` (shipped-behavior defects) — redistributed the epic's
loose leaves into them alongside the existing G4, G5 and security sub-epics, and
moved `RURL-kptzgkii` (release plan) out to `RURL-haqmdhbh`. The root's
placement is unchanged, so D-C.1 is not engaged.

No snapshot is taken for this change. The manifest continues to record
`sha256: cb188cf3925f008e0f888ecf6e389590d58aac5e733a1c0bdb84a4189154b746`,
`issue_count: 69`, `as_of_commit: v3/cp-snapshot-3`, and the on-disk export
still hashes to that value. These new leaves are recorded against the frozen
export delta per P0.3 §3 (lines 88-89), unchanged.

## Consequences

- **G0.2 / G0.4** are unaffected: both read the frozen export and the manifest,
  neither of which this record changes. The restore drill's `issue_count`
  comparison still passes at 69.
- **No gate is re-opened and no hash cascades.** This record adds a file under
  `decisions/`; the manifest's `decisions:` list gains one row at acceptance,
  which is the ordinary cost of any new record.

  the work reveals better groupings, without a record per cut.

# P0.5 — G4 exit-criterion scope: verified-where-shipped with named-carrier deferral

## Decision (PROPOSED)

> This record is the agent's **proposed** disposition, offered for the owner to
> ratify, reject, or adjust. Nothing here is binding until the owner merges it
> (P0.1 §4). The substance below is the owner's decision as stated in the
> 2026-07-25 review; this record makes it durable and executable.

### The revised criterion (concrete)

§7 G4 criterion 3 currently reads:

> Full-string, state, vector, mutation, join, cache, determinism, and migration
> properties have positive and negative coverage.

It is **refined**, not replaced, to:

> Full-string, state, vector, mutation, join, cache, determinism, and migration
> properties have positive and negative coverage **for every cell whose subject
> surface is shipped**. A cell whose subject surface is specified but not
> shipped **must be explicitly deferred to a named carrier**. A deferred cell is
> **unsatisfied**, not met.

The criterion's substance is unchanged. What changes is the **admissible
disposition of a cell**: previously a cell was covered or it was a gap; now a
cell is covered, or deferred-with-carrier, or a gap — and deferral is a
recorded, gated state rather than an absence.

### Deferral is not satisfaction

A deferred cell **remains unsatisfied**. It is never counted toward coverage,
never reported as met, and never contributes to a PASS. Deferral changes only
*whether G4 may exit while the cell is open* — it does not change the cell's
verification status. Any tally that presents deferred cells as covered is a
false statement of the gate's result and is itself a defect.

### The three failure conditions

The gate **fails closed** when any of the following holds:

1. **Missing carrier.** A cell is deferred and names no carrier. (This mirrors
   the release gate's R6 rule, which already fails when a deferred criterion
   names no carrier — `verification/release-slice.md`.)
2. **Carrier discharged without satisfaction.** A carrier is recorded as
   resolved while the cell it carries is still unverified. Closing the carrier
   is not evidence about the cell.
3. **Silent graduation.** The subject surface ships while the cell is still
   deferred. Building the surface discharges the *reason* for deferral, so
   verification must follow; a shipped-but-still-deferred cell is a gate
   failure, not a pending item.

Condition 3 is the load-bearing one. Conditions 1 and 2 keep deferrals honest;
condition 3 is what stops "specified-but-unshipped" from becoming a permanent
parking space that the surface quietly escapes.

### The authoritative substrate is a register, not the tracker

P0.1 §5 makes tracker state **non-authoritative**: "Nothing stated solely in the
tracker is normative." A gate may therefore **not** read failure condition 2
from an `fp` issue's status.

Deferrals are recorded in a tracked, append-only register —
`registers/verification-deferrals.md` — whose rows are the authority. Each row
carries the cell id, the owning artifact, the surface whose absence justifies
the deferral, the carrier id, and the row state. The `fp` id is a **pointer**
for human navigation; the register row is what the gate reads and what governs.
This is the same separation the determinism-exceptions register already uses.

Failure condition 3 is executable precisely because "the surface ships" is a
fact about the repository (an export appearing in `NAMESPACE`, a function
appearing in `R/`), not a fact about the tracker.

### Reporting posture

G4's result is reported as **partial verification with named deferrals** — never
as full contract coverage. Its acceptance record must state the covered count,
the deferred count, and the carrier for every deferral, in a form where the two
cannot be read as one number. G5's independent re-review consumes that posture,
not a headline PASS.

### What this record does not do

- It does **not** relax, reinterpret, or reopen any contract cell. Every cell
  keeps the meaning its owning G3 artifact gives it.
- It does **not** decide whether any unbuilt surface should be built, or when.
  That is product authority (P1–P5 tiers) and is carried per-surface.
- It does **not** touch criteria 1 (claim traceability) or 2 (labeled oracles).
  Both are met by construction once the verification slices land.

### Standing-mandate check (correctness beats back-compat)

The owner's standing mandate prefers spec-exactness to preserved output. This
record is consistent with it: nothing here weakens a contract or preserves a
behavior. It narrows only what a *gate* may claim, in the direction of claiming
less. Where the audit found shipped behavior contradicting a SETTLED cell, this
record does not bless the contradiction — those cases are behavior-change work
carried separately, and their cells stay unsatisfied until the behavior changes.

## Rationale

**The criterion as written cannot be satisfied, and pretending otherwise is the
failure mode.** A six-family read-only audit (state, mutation, full-string,
vector, migration, join) mapped the contract cells of each family onto the
shipped suite. Two findings force this record:

1. **Several cells specify surfaces that do not exist.** `get_url_key()`,
   `url_key_policy()`, the six `url_*_join()` operations, `serialize_url()`,
   `format_url()`, the three verdict-layer fields, and P1.2's
   `authority_delimiter_present` / `authority_payload_kind` are absent from
   `NAMESPACE` and `R/`. For the join family this is 37 of 75 cells; for
   full-string it is 10 of 43 in-scope cells. **No test can close them.** The
   only dispositions available are "defer with a carrier" or "block G4 on
   building v3's remaining surface area."
2. **The shipped half is genuinely well covered.** state is 35/44 cells covered
   in both polarities, mutation 23/45 with exactly one zero-evidence cell,
   migration 8 of 14 testable cells. The evidence is real and currently mapped
   to nothing. Blocking G4 wholesale would strand it.

**G4 is an evidence gate, not an implementation gate.** §7 sequences G0–G5 as
durability → inventory → authority → contract matrix → *evidence* → re-review.
Requiring G4 to prove properties of unbuilt surfaces would make it an
implementation gate by side effect, and would defer all evidence work behind all
remaining construction — inverting the sequence the protocol chose.

**The alternative was considered and rejected.** Blocking G4 until the key
surface, the standards serializer, `format_url()`, and the verdict layers ship
is coherent, but it is equivalent to blocking the evidence gate on building v3
itself, and it would leave the strong existing evidence unmapped and
unprotected in the meantime. Deferral-with-carrier keeps the unbuilt cells
visibly unsatisfied while letting the built cells be verified now.

**Why a register and not the tracker.** P0.1 §5 already settled that trackers
carry no authority. A gate that read `fp` status would make the tracker
normative through the back door and would break the moment an issue was
retitled, reparented, or bulk-closed. The register is tracked, reviewed, and
hashed like every other artifact.

## Consequences

1. **Every verification slice must classify, not merely count.** A cell is
   covered (both polarities), deferred (with carrier), or a gap. "Positive-only"
   is not a disposition — it is a gap in the negative polarity and must be
   reported as such.
2. **A new register and gate are required** before G4 can exit. They ship in
   this slice.
3. **The gate is fail-closed and self-tested**, matching the three gates already
   in the verify chain (C-08 cache-doc consistency, C-09 determinism, C-10
   release rule). It must be demonstrated to FAIL on an injected violation, not
   merely to pass.
4. **Condition 3 creates a standing obligation.** When any deferred surface
   ships, its cells must be verified in the same change or the gate goes red.
   This is intentional: it converts "we'll verify it later" into a build-time
   failure.
5. **G4's acceptance record cannot claim full coverage**, and G5's reviewer is
   entitled to read the deferral register as the list of what G4 did not prove.
